# Professional review handoff

This is an optional handoff, not a launch requirement. The operator clarified
that ELN should present official rules and relevant actual case studies, without
requiring human legal sign-off. Continue source-backed publication within that
scope and keep review descriptions accurate.

ELN's published legal content has AI-assisted source review. No professional
legal review, attorney identity, engagement or legal sign-off has been recorded.
The site continues to disclose that limitation. Software work cannot provide
the missing human professional review.

`professional-review-register.csv` is the concrete handoff: each canonical rule,
its actual requirement and limits, primary-source URL/passage/access status,
substantive review date and affected published guide paths. Empty human-review
columns are deliberate. Regenerate with `node scripts/review-handoff.mjs` after
merging a reviewed content batch.

Review priorities are federal/state franchise and business-opportunity
classification; disclosure/payment timing; filing and exemption conditions;
employment/immigration/tax boundaries; and accuracy and privacy obligations for
the lead forms and private board. Reviewers should distinguish current enacted
law, agency guidance, historical litigation, allegations, proposed changes and
unresolved conflicts. A fetched page or a technical hash is not legal sign-off.

The reviewer should record their real name and qualification, scope, date,
decision and correction evidence. Resolve missing or blocked primary authorities
before clearing a statement that depends on them. A deliberately limited page
should retain its actual limits. Approval of one rule must not mark an entire
state, country or topic as professionally reviewed.

Use the proposed-edit workflow in `EDITORIAL.md` for corrections, update all
affected pages and tools, and run validation before publication. Professional
review counts and public wording may change only after actual completed human
review evidence is retained. No engagement, payment or third-party contact has
been initiated on the operator's behalf.
