# Geographic scope and first research batch

Reviewed 12 November 2026. Reviewer status throughout: AI-assisted source review; no professional legal review.

## Origin convention

The core geographical convention is UN Statistics Division M49, read from its current country/area table. Europe is not treated as the European Union. The register includes:

- 44 sovereign-country records in M49 Europe, including Russia in full for this statistical convention.
- All 11 South-eastern Asian countries: Brunei, Cambodia, Indonesia, Laos, Malaysia, Myanmar, Philippines, Singapore, Thailand, Timor-Leste and Vietnam.
- Seven separate supplemental records: Armenia, Azerbaijan, Cyprus, Georgia, Kazakhstan, Türkiye and Kosovo. The first six are outside M49 Europe; Cyprus remains relevant as an EU member. Kosovo is tracked for planning without making a political-recognition determination.
- Eight separately labelled dependency/area records: Åland Islands, Faroe Islands, Gibraltar, Guernsey, Isle of Man, Jersey, Svalbard and Jan Mayen, and Greenland. Greenland is M49 Northern America, rather than Europe.

Total: **70 origin records**. Overseas French, Dutch and other linked territories outside this geographical grouping are not silently included under a metropolitan country. If a business is actually incorporated or operates there, add a separate record and legal workstream before applying parent-country assumptions. Inclusion in the origin register establishes no treaty, sanctions, tax or immigration conclusion.

An origin record does not combine parent incorporation, headquarters, actual operations, ultimate owners' nationality, tax residence, U.S. selling entity, buyer residence, offer locations, outlet locations, imported products or employee work locations. These stay separate in planning factsheets.

Five scoped published origin guides cover Singapore management/people separation, UK services/supply facts, Dutch industrial-service partner/territory evidence, French partner-data access, and Malaysian ownership tracing. These are narrow, conditional topic records; none makes a country fully researched across all ten topic families.

## Local market scope

The register contains **40 municipal candidates**, with **30 narrowly researched local process records** and **10 unresearched/blocked candidates**. A researched process is not full local coverage. Every researched market remains `conditional`; the actual address, county responsibilities, special districts, sector rules, employment, local/state tax and site approvals remain separately unresolved.

The initial selection spans Pacific, Pacific Northwest, Southwest, Mountain, Texas, Great Lakes, Northeast, Mid-Atlantic, Midwest and Southeast commercial regions. These are comparison labels, not legal jurisdictions, and no market is called “best.” Selection used B2B/management-run relevance, geographic breadth and accessible primary municipal instructions. It is not a quantitative demand ranking.

Each record names the municipality and county or possible counties. Multi-county municipalities require parcel-level allocation. Independent cities and consolidated city/counties are identified explicitly. Metro names and postal addresses are never used as proof of municipal jurisdiction. In particular, Las Vegas city, the unincorporated Strip, Richmond city versus nearby counties, and St. Louis city versus St. Louis County are distinct.

The 30 reviewed processes include tax/registration steps, trade-name filings, category screening, occupancy triggers and city/county sequencing. Ten distinct local guides and one Dallas/San Antonio comparison are published; other markets remain structured coverage records rather than cloned SEO pages.

## Source review and limitations

Direct official pages were fetched and read. Seattle used official-page web retrieval after the direct request was blocked. Search snippets were used for discovery only. The failed Indianapolis fetch exposed only a JavaScript shell and is not counted as researched. Several guessed URLs were stale and were replaced only after finding and reading current official guidance. Successful HTTP status by itself was not counted as substantive verification.

The Sacramento initial page fetch was too large and exposed navigation without the substantive body. The published process instead uses the fully read official Small Business Guide. The EDPB's 2024 DPF FAQ is explicitly superseded; the current January 2026 version 2 PDF was fetched and read in full. The PDF adoption date (15 January 2026), web publication date (23 January 2026), fetch time and substantive verification time are recorded separately where known.

Source issues retained for editorial follow-up:

1. Enterprise Singapore's U.S. doing-business page says the USSFTA entered into force in January 2014. That date is excluded from publication. The U.S. International Trade Administration’s implementation note was fetched and read and expressly confirms January 1, 2004; its source record resolves the historical-date discrepancy without making a current tariff claim.
2. KVK's broad W-8BEN-E statement could be read as an automatic withholding exemption. Published content instead uses the IRS instructions and leaves payment source, treaty and limitation-on-benefits conditions open. Its VAT/sales-tax shorthand is not adopted.
3. KVK and UK export pages contain volatile tariff information. No numeric tariff from those broad guides is inserted into the site's cost model or assumed to apply to a product.
4. USCIS and FAM pages were inaccessible during the geography review. Malaysia/Singapore mobility explanations reuse the shared federal team's current CFR rule records; no consular or escrow finding is made.
5. Dallas’s occupancy page uses inconsistent wording on DBA-only changes: an introductory record-change route and a later FAQ describing a standalone CO application/new CO. No fee or automatic shortcut is published; confirm the current workflow with Dallas. The unresolved conflict is linked to both the city guide and Dallas/San Antonio comparison.
6. The French CNIL overview is read alongside the newer EDPB FAQ. Certification, legal-entity scope, HR data and Article 28 contracts are kept distinct.

Sources are linked, with short supporting passages and separate fetch/verification dates. Full fetched text in `research/geography/` is a working research artefact, not content automatically approved for republication. Remove or exclude these working extracts from a source distribution if its rights/retention policy requires it; public pages contain original summaries and short excerpts only.
