# ELN research continuation

This release follows the owner-recovery/backup release documented in
`OPERATIONS-RELEASE.md`. Its reviewed public corpus contains 278 canonical guides,
640 source records, 390 canonical rules and nine planning tools. That is 103 more
published guides than the 175-guide release. See `coverage-report.json` for the
machine-generated counts and definitions.

All 703 original editorial candidates have an explicit disposition: 182 published
destinations, 202 scoped merges, 73 retired page concepts, nine implemented tools
and 237 further-research leads. A merge or retirement is an editorial decision,
not a finding that independent law has been researched or does not apply. Five
proposed tools remain unimplemented; they are not counted as working tools.
The original 603 count conflated unpublished paths with research work remaining;
the current report separates those concepts.

The work adds country-specific primary evidence across 48 published origin
guides, independent state franchise/relationship explanations, narrow industry
and municipal operating guides, current federal planning requirements and a
51-jurisdiction payroll-account comparison. The 50 states and D.C. have separate
withholding and unemployment scopes; unknown account requirements remain unknown.
No state receives full employment, tax, sector or local clearance from a payroll
account check. Territory and address-dependent operating questions remain
independent in the public coverage register.

Readers can filter the library to documented cases. Relevant rules and selected
management guides link to the five published actual-case pages: Qargo, Burgerim,
Tower, Alacrity and the 2026 PFG/Franchise FastLane matter. Allegations, admissions,
historical default/consent judgments and proposed settlements are distinguished.
The PFG court documents remain proposed filings with blank judicial signature
fields; no entered judgment or payment recovery is claimed. Actual case source
changes identify the guides repeating those facts in case cards.

The four original holds remain evidence-dependent: the California core guide and
OctoClean order, Mississippi's full current statutory/case branch, and Laos's
current complete translated currency rules. The Ukrainian outward-funding guide
is also withheld after inaccessible current primary material. Narrow other
California operating guides do not release its blocked franchise hub. Held
content is absent from public article routes and the sitemap.

The operator clarified that qualified-human legal sign-off is not required for
this product: official rules and relevant actual cases are the requested scope.

review, transaction approval or professional qualification is invented. The
professional review CSV is an optional future handoff only.
