## Actionable clarifications

1. **Oregon defined seller — scope wording.** Both `/states/oregon/` and `/states/oregon/renewal-and-additional-unit-disclosure/` use wording like “defines a franchisor as including a subfranchisor selling for $100 or more.” ORS 650.005(6) instead defines a person, including a subfranchisor, who sells a franchise for $100 or more to a franchisee or subfranchisor. Rephrase so the dollar condition clearly qualifies the defined seller generally rather than appearing to modify only subfranchisors. Do not turn this into a universal low-fee exemption. Evidence: `research/raw/batch3/state4/or-official-web-open.txt`, complete definitions around reader lines271–289. Root and the state agent were notified.
2. **Literal Unicode escapes in the filing comparison.** Record `b3-state3-registration-vs-notice` contains literal `\u2019` sequences in its JSON string values, including Michigan/Indiana paragraphs and the fictional example. Those would display as escape text. Decode the intended apostrophes during integration without changing source IDs, paths or legal conditions. Root was notified. This is a presentation defect rather than a legal rule finding.
