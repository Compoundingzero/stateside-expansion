## Retrieval and currency

Public official bodies were fetched through the environment's inherited HTTP proxy and certificate trust. Raw responses and attempt metadata remain ignored under `research/raw/batch3/origin2/`. Fetching and substantive review dates are separate; the authoring helper retains a fixed review timestamp so regeneration cannot fabricate a fresh legal review.

Several initial guessed English URLs were stale, and a Czech guessed path returned an HTTP200 not-found page. A guessed Portuguese foreign-filing path returned the service directory rather than the requested process. None counted as evidence. Correct process pages were found through the authorities' actual public navigation and read before use.

The Latvian official English statute expressly says its translation is outdated, valid for 2024 and missing later amendments. That is a genuine currency block for the English text. Current rules use the corresponding native Latvian consolidation, including the relevant current zero-rate conditions and exceptions, summarised in English with AI assistance. The Cabinet documentary list remains an explicit research queue item rather than an invented checklist of sufficient documents.

Polish, Portuguese, Latvian and Lithuanian passages are quoted in their original language with AI-assisted English explanations. The VMI page contains a large navigation menu and several independently dated answers: only the named answers and the relevant complete table were used. The Polish FAQ is similarly large; its named scope, foreign-recipient, online visualisation/QR and supporting-document answers were read in context, without claiming a review of every unrelated system rule.

Guichet's January 2023 update dates are retained in source notes and guide/coverage limits. The guide uses a narrow other-EU reporting boundary and receiving-establishment mapping; it deliberately avoids relying on the older event/digital/B2C examples or declaring that all later statutory amendments were checked. Source update/revision dates are not relabelled as identified publication dates.

No government service was logged into. No invoice, registration, trademark filing, data-breach notice, tax return or other external submission was made.
