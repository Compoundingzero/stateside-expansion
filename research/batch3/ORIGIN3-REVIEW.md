## Material source findings

**Malta:** The first retrieved hashed statute PDF listed amendments only through 2022. The canonical `https://legislation.mt/eli/cap/406/eng/pdf` lists Act III of 2026 and Legal Notices 75 and 86 of 2026 and includes Article 12(3a), absent from the older PDF. Cited current provisions were read from the canonical version, including Articles 12(3), 12(3a), 20(2) and the Third Schedule general rule and relevant exceptions. MTCA’s notes say publication December 23, 2025 and last update February 6, 2025, while PDF metadata records creation February 6, 2026. The conflict is not silently corrected. The notes expressly disclaim binding Article 75 status; the primary law supplies the legal scope.

**Iceland:** Current Alþingi Article 12(1), point 2 has a business-recipient rule with local-service exceptions; point 3 is a different other-recipient track. The English agency explanation and old government translation of Regulation 194/1990 use older listed-service/use/input-credit language. That older language is not substituted for the current B2B statutory text, and its two-year certificate provision is not published as current. The current regulation page returned 403 through both direct and ordinary public web retrieval. The current Parliament body is accessible and was read for the guide’s narrow statutory scope.

**Croatia:** The older AZOP article says updated September 6, 2022 despite displaying current related-news widgets. A later actual AZOP article explains the July 2023 EU-U.S. framework and participating-recipient condition. The current Commission list corroborates the qualified U.S. entry. The article dates, old Privacy Shield mechanism and later framework are kept separate.

**Bulgaria:** The live national authorization service provides forms under the ministerial order dated May 21, 2026 and references Regulation 2021/821, but retains Regulation 428/2009 in an older re-export paragraph and inconsistent fee descriptions. The 2024 official law compilation was read as a dated primary document, not falsely described as a fully reconciled 2026 code. The guide supports a conditional packet-preparation decision, leaving classification, current substantive requirements, general authorizations, fees and actual permission open.

**Romania:** Direct latest consolidated-law routes returned 502. An older agency-organization decree and older statutory version could be read through public web retrieval, but they were not mislabeled as the current trademark law. The published proposal uses the actual readable OSIM FAQ only for its company/domain/trademark distinction, national territorial scope and evidence tasks. It omits procedural deadlines, fees and definitive international-route requirements.
