## Focused corrections and additions

1. **Alaska hub replacement.** The public print route returned the complete AS 45.66 chapter, rather than the default navigation shell. Section .220(2) expressly excludes “a franchise under 16 C.F.R. Part 436” from this chapter. It is not replaced here by an invented state notice, fee or material-compliance condition. Other .220 branches and .070 connections remain factual questions. The complete 9 AAC 16 body expressly incorporates February 9, 2005 forms: their age alone is not evidence of obsolescence. Nonexempt registration must be complete at least 30 days before offers/sales begin; completion depends on received acknowledgement, rather than merely sending an application. The 10-calendar-day disclosure, anniversary renewal, 10-day material-change and three-year post-last-sale bond clocks are separate. The 30-day cancellation and nonwaiver discussion is explicitly for nonexempt chapter contracts. The statutory compilation label and incomplete later-session-law audit are visible on the page and in coverage; no complete 2026 Alaska-law opinion is claimed.
2. **Louisiana hub replacement.** Corrects `Glimits` spacing. Makes the existing RS 51:1823(6) written material oral-promises and (7) no-waiver branch explicit. Adds the actual full §1822(H) continuous bond text, with subsection (G) coverage. The 60-day notice is the surety's bond-termination process, not a franchise-contract termination deadline. Current full section fetched and read; no generic contract-renewal duty is inferred.
3. **Maine hub replacement.** Separates material disclosure updates under §4696 from one-year effectiveness and annual renewal under §4697. The source is the saved complete official chapter, current through October 1, 2026 and expressly not the certified annotated text. No new fee estimate or blanket franchise exception is introduced.
4. **Nevada hub replacement.** Actual NRS 597.1143(1) covers substantial term changes as well as termination/nonrenewal; .115(4) and .118 add uncontrollable-circumstance and nonwaiver boundaries. The 180-day notice and 60-day correction language in .1143(2)–(3) is not turned into a universal amendment clock. Farm-equipment inventory and dealer agreement conditions remain explicit.
5. **Existing Nebraska relationship page replacement.** No new page: `/states/nebraska/franchise-relationship-scope/` already existed. The added paragraph states all three §87-403 conditions accurately: qualifying Nebraska place, covered sales between parties exceeding $35,000 in the 12 months preceding institution of suit, and more than 20 percent of the franchisee's sales intended or derived from the franchise. These are not a business-size floor. It also retains §87-404(1)'s express reservation for nonrenewability or reasonable contractual renewal conditions, rather than implying indefinite renewal from good cause alone. Sections 87-402–404 were freshly retrieved and compared with the saved primary bodies.
