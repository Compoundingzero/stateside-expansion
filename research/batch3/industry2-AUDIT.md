No existing canonical guide/candidate array, package, Git state or deployment was changed by this proposal authoring step. The proposal builder edits only these four industry2 arrays; it does not conduct a new review or advance the review date when regenerated.

## Evidence and source-access boundaries

Bodies and retrieval metadata are retained under the ignored `research/raw/batch3/industry2/` directory. The fetch audit files record successful and unsuccessful attempts. HTTP 404/403/503, an HTTP-200 error/bootstrap page or an unreviewed search excerpt is not treated as researched legal evidence.

- The 2026 CSLB law book was retrieved as an actual PDF and the relevant complete BPC 7026, 7028(a) and 7048 passages were read. The minor-work branch requires **less than $1,000** aggregate/all items plus its other conditions; the larger-project/splitting, contractor-advertising and employed-helper exclusions remain visible. This is not a finding that every ordinary gardening visit is contractor work. Only the cited statutory sections, not the full law book, are claimed reviewed.
- Current 49 CFR 387.307, including its January 2026 effective text and July 2026 amendment context, replaces reliance on an older FMCSA portal/timing page. The $75,000 amount is bond/trust security, not a quoted premium, application fee or complete operating budget.
- The IRS PTIN page is in the 2027 application cycle, updated October 2026. The guide calls for the applicable preparation-year record and publishes neither the displayed fee nor a false universal 2026 renewal claim. A failed follow-up FAQ is not claimed read.
- The NYC boarding portal contains legacy Silverlight/renewal text and conflicting fee material. Only supported substantive permit, manager and inspection statements are used, together with the relevant actual Health Code provisions. Current technical workflow and numeric fees remain unresolved.
- Local requests for BAR `/apply` and `/wir` returned upstream failures. The federal research agent recovered the actual complete readable official pages through the public reader; the relevant full branches were also read by this author. Exports `bar-reader-1.json` and `bar-reader-2.json` preserve that representation. The January 2026 Write It Right maintenance qualifications and commercial-agreement/roadside scope boundaries are retained. - The current official 15 USC 2068 reader body expressly states currency through Public Law 119-124 of 15 October 2026. Its specified corrective-action/order/ban conditions and subsection (b) qualifications are retained, rather than asserting an unlimited rule about any informal recall. The actual CPSC resale FAQ body is separately preserved. The broader product-safety/reporting guide is reused conceptually rather than duplicating its rules; this guide answers item intake and release.
- These four reader sources use day-precision fetch/review metadata and **do not claim a raw HTML hash**. The CPSC guide's introduction/basic/recall pages were consulted by the reviewer, but the 89-page guide is not included as an entirely read source or used to imply full review.
- Actual NYC discharge conditions were not recovered from the attempted DEP locations. The laundry guide therefore frames the federal receiving-authority/SIU decision without inventing a permit outcome. The separate acidified-food process section 21 CFR 114.83 was not retrieved; it remains an express scope gap rather than an implied process clearance.
