## What the actual status sources establish

The May 1, 2025 Field Assistance Bulletin 2025-1 expressly directs WHD investigators to a different approach in its specified scope, says the 2024 analysis remains in effect for private litigation, and says the bulletin does not change rights/responsibilities under the FLSA. The February 27, 2026 Federal Register document is a proposed rescission/replacement and proposed FMLA/MSPA extension, not an effective replacement. The actual dated Federal Register search response and corresponding official published PDF were saved.

The bulletin's original PDF cites FLSA2019-6 while the 2026 proposal refers to FLSA2025-2 in its description. This is not silently normalized into an opinion holding. The guide does not apply either opinion. IRS federal employment-tax control categories were separately read and are not substituted for FLSA/state tests.

The actual Colt & Joe Trucking district opinion/order was obtained: Document 49, January 9, 2025, 37 pages. Its conclusion finds no standing and alternatively rejects/identifies waiver of arguments. The latest appellate dockets were **not independently queried**; this is residual research, not a claimed access block or proof of no later action.

### Joint employment
