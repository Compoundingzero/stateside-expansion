# Industry and case readability rewrite: frozen handoff

All 48 assigned articles are rewritten in `industries_cases-output.json`: 35 industry guides, eight fictional cases and five real cases. The file is frozen for root integration.

Each patch contains only the contract's fields: matching ID/path, title, description, a 2–4 sentence learning summary, 2–6 defined terms, individually rewritten sections, practical output, limitations and reading time. All other fields—including dates, legal/source metadata, scenario type, labels, source/rule arrays, status and jurisdiction—remain outside the patch and must be preserved during integration. No canonical data, source or implementation file was edited for this task.

The revisions explain the business situation and responsible people before introducing statutes. They define the relevant agencies and specialist words, explain the distinction that changes the decision, and expand the fictional exercises into a sequence of reasoning and concrete questions. Planning records are described as optional business aids rather than invented legal forms. The Burgerim article now uses “Written record of sales promises,” replacing “Sales-claim register.”
