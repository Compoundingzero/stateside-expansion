# FTC teaching reference review

The user asked that the released articles explain U.S. expansion rules for an
intelligent reader without prior legal training. The revision uses the FTC's
Franchise Rule overview, official Compliance Guide landing page and complete
154-page May 2008 PDF as explanatory references. Its introduction expressly
explains that the guide does not amend the rule and is staff guidance.

The current eCFR human-facing Part 436 page returned its automated-access notice.
The advertised official API was used, with inherited runtime networking, to
retrieve the complete current Part 436 XML. Metadata reported the title current
through 2026-11-10. The entire normalized text of §436.5 is identical to the
previously source-reviewed copy. Raw XML and request metadata are retained under
`research/raw/readability/`, outside Git. No CAPTCHA, cookies or access challenge
was solved or bypassed.

The explanatory structure is: business context, words defined, rule explained,
worked reasoning, practical preparation. In particular:

- Explain franchisor, franchisee and FDD before discussing numbered items.
- Separate the disclosure duty from government approval and business success.
- Separate federal disclosures from independent state/territorial and operating
  duties; a licence or disclosure receipt does not complete all of those tasks.
- Preserve the definition/exemption distinction and current $735 payment
  exemption, rather than copying the old PDF's $500 figure.
- Distinguish a required disclosure item from the optional choice to make a
  financial performance representation in Item 19.
- Explain financial terms, company responsibility, manager costs and comparisons
  through explicitly fictional examples rather than unexplained shorthand.

This is an editorial teaching revision. Article modification dates will change;
source-review dates and recorded uncertainty are not automatically refreshed.
No new human qualifications, case outcomes or legal clearances are claimed.
