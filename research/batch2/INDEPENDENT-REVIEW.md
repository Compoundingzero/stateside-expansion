## Changes required before publication

1. **Readable prose.** The initial `west-own-guides.json` contained pervasive concatenated words, including Utah “proofofnoticereceipt” and Ohio “materiallyFTC-compliant”. The east authoring source also showed “Chapter551Adisclosure” and “isNovember12,2026”. These findings were sent to the publication owner while authors were still working.

2. **NC/SC location limb must keep its equipment scope.** North Carolina §66-94(1) and South Carolina §39-57-20(1) concern locations for specified vending/rack/display/amusement devices on the stated third-party premises. Broad references to “locations” can make a B2B reader mistake any customer-account promise for that limb. Name the device-location scope. Keep the fictional cleaning examples conditional on the precise income/refund promise and the statute’s payment facts; do not infer classification from a generic “income guarantee” label. Apply the same precise device-location qualifier to Louisiana RS51:1821(1)(a) where its guide summarises “location assistance”. These are separate from Ohio’s broader account/outlet limb. [NC Article 19](https://house.ncleg.gov/EnactedLegislation/Statutes/HTML/ByArticle/Chapter_66/Article_19.html), [SC Chapter 57](https://www.scstatehouse.gov/code/t39c057).

3. **Ohio proof burden needs procedural context.** §1334.14 places the exemption/exception burden on the claimant in cases under §1334.08 or §1334.09. Add that scope to the proposed sentence and matrix summary rather than presenting it as an unqualified universal burden rule. The preserved guarantee/buy-back branches in §1334.13 were verified independently. [Ohio Chapter 1334](https://codes.ohio.gov/ohio-revised-code/chapter-1334).

4. **SC earnings sentence.** The initial guide said §39-57-60 “restricts substantiated earnings statements”. State the actual proposition: earnings claims require documented substantiation disclosed when the representation is made. The original wording could invert or obscure the condition. [SC Chapter 57](https://www.scstatehouse.gov/code/t39c057).

5. **Thai receipt deadline precision.** The current Thai BOT summary states the sale/deposit period from acquisition **or bringing-in**, not only the older English “receipt” wording. Preserve the alternatives and have the actual transaction/date classified. Do not infer a safe harbour at exactly USD10 million from the press release’s shorthand. The origin author subsequently confirmed this correction, including a bank-confirmation task. The current Thai source, older English source and dated statement should remain separately recorded. [Current Thai BOT summary](https://www.bot.or.th/th/our-roles/financial-markets/foreign-exchange-regulations/foreign-exchange-laws.html).

The publication owner must confirm that these changes survive the final canonical merge; this note does not automatically mark a finding resolved.
