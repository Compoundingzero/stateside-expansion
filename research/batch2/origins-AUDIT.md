## Publication safeguards and findings

Brunei is E-1-only in the reviewed State table; no Brunei E-2 entitlement is asserted. Cambodia/Indonesia are not silently assumed to be E-treaty countries. Tax residence, company incorporation, ultimate nationality, operating location and work location remain separate fields.

Thailand's current Thai BOT summary and dated 20 January 2026 release use USD 10 million, while the English summary still displays USD 1 million. Guides preserve this conflict, label AI-assisted translation, distinguish summaries from unreviewed operative instruments, retain acquisition OR bringing-in alternatives for the second 360-day trigger, and require bank-confirmed dates/categories/exceptions. Exactly USD 10 million is not described as exempt. The threshold is not added to any calculator.

Spain's official 2026 ICEX Next extract closes the application window on 30 October 2026 at 14:00. It is explicitly closed as of this review; no future call, grant award or universal SME revenue definition is invented.

Myanmar guidance uses current 31 CFR §§525.201,525.405–407,525.411–412 and525.502, OFAC's ownership explanation and current programme page. It distinguishes targeted property/actor/ownership prohibitions from a blanket nationality ban, and list screening/licences from banking policy. No actual screening/clearance is claimed.

The Philippine BSP source currently serves a consolidation labelled updated May 2025. The receipt/outward-investment distinction is explained without quoting an unverified current numerical threshold; later circulars and bank documentation are explicit tasks.

Vietnam's posted 2015 agreement is not treated as evidence of current treaty benefits. The Treasury document-collection entry and absent Vietnam row in the reviewed IRS index create a status/entitlement evidence task, not a fabricated tax rate.

Laos stays unpublished. Older official portal rules could not be reconciled with a later inaccessible BOL 2025 PDF; goods/services registration wording is also inconsistent between metadata and the instrument. Exact unblock actions are in the draft. No 180-day current-rule claim is published.

German coverage-certificate preparation remains separate from visas, work permission, income tax and wider employment duties. Irish/Italian agency support and introductions remain preparation/lead sources, never partner certification.
